About Milton, Florida

City Information

  • Founded: 1844
  • Population (2020): 10,197
  • Average Annual High: 99°F
  • Average Annual Low: 17°F
  • Total Area: 5.96 square miles
  • Government: Council-Manager System

Welcome to Milton, a vibrant city nestled in the heart of Santa Rosa County, Florida! As the county seat, Milton proudly serves as the administrative and cultural hub of the region. This charming city, established in 1844, has grown from its historic roots into a modern community while preserving its rich heritage.

Milton's strategic location in the Florida Panhandle offers residents and visitors the best of both worlds: the tranquility of small-town living combined with easy access to urban amenities. The city's landscape is a beautiful tapestry of natural wonders, featuring pristine waterways, lush forests, and stunning coastal areas just a short drive away.

The city operates under a Council-Manager form of government, ensuring efficient and responsive local governance. With a population of over 10,000 residents, Milton maintains a close-knit community feel while offering all the conveniences of modern living. The city's climate, characterized by warm summers and mild winters, makes it an ideal location for outdoor activities year-round.

Milton's commitment to preserving its historical character while embracing progress is evident in its well-maintained historic districts and modern developments. The city's location provides easy access to major transportation routes, making it a convenient base for exploring the beautiful Florida Panhandle region.
