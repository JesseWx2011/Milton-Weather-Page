Storm Details

Hurricane Milton was the most intense hurricane ever recorded in the Gulf of Mexico (tied with Rita 2005). The storm underwent rapid intensification, dropping from 977 mb to 895 mb in just 17 hours, and peaked as a Category 5 hurricane with 180 mph winds. Milton made landfall near Siesta Key, Florida, as a Category 3 hurricane on October 9, 2024, at 8:30 PM EDT. The storm caused $34.4 billion in damage, 45 fatalities (42 US, 3 Mexico), and spawned a record tornado outbreak in Florida. Its track and intensity were well forecast by most guidance, and timely warnings were issued for affected areas.
