Hurricane Preparedness Actions

Before the Storm

Hurricane Katrina (2005) - A reminder of the importance of early preparation and evacuation.

  • Create an emergency plan for your family
  • Build an emergency supply kit
  • Know your evacuation zone and routes
  • Secure your home and property
  • Stay informed about weather conditions

During the Storm

Storm Surge in St. Petersburg (2023) - Demonstrates the dangerous impact of coastal flooding.

  • Stay indoors and away from windows
  • Listen to local officials and weather updates
  • Use flashlights instead of candles
  • Keep emergency supplies readily accessible
  • Stay in a safe room or interior space

After the Storm

Inside Hurricane Jose (2017) - NOAA Hurricane Hunters' view from inside the eye of the storm.

  • Wait for official clearance before returning home
  • Check for structural damage
  • Be cautious of downed power lines
  • Document any damage for insurance
  • Help neighbors if it's safe to do so
