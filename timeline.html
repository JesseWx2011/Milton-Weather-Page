Website Timeline

The Evolution of Milton Weather Dashboard

April 10th, 2025
🚀 Official Launch
Milton Weather Dashboard officially launches, marking the beginning of a comprehensive weather monitoring platform for the Florida Panhandle.
April 11th, 2025
🌅 Sunrise/Sunset Feature
Added interactive sunrise and sunset timeline feature with visual sun icons and day length calculations.
April 11th, 2025
📹 Weather Cameras
Integrated live weather camera feeds to provide real-time visual weather conditions from multiple locations.
April 13th, 2025
⚙️ Units Feature Temporarily Removed
Units feature temporarily removed due to non-functional status. Users continued with default imperial measurements.
April 16th, 2025
🌙 Moon Phases
Added comprehensive moon phase tracking with upcoming lunar events and phase information.
April 23rd, 2025
🔧 Major Site Fix
Resolved a critical issue that prevented the website from loading properly, ensuring uninterrupted service.
April 24th, 2025
📊 UV/Solar Data
Added UV index and solar radiation monitoring capabilities to track sun exposure and radiation levels.
April 24th, 2025
🔢 Visitor Counter Replacement
Site visitor counter malfunctioned and was replaced with a new, more reliable system that remains active as of June 2025.
April 25th, 2025
🌪️ Radar Card
Added weather radar integration with live radar imagery from KMOB station for real-time storm tracking.
April 28th, 2025
📅 Forecast Page
Launched dedicated forecast page with extended weather predictions and detailed meteorological data.
May 3rd, 2025
⚖️ Unit Switcher Re-added
Successfully restored the unit conversion feature, allowing users to switch between imperial and metric measurements.
May 3rd, 2025
⚡ Lightning Data Card
Added lightning strike monitoring with daily counts and last strike timestamps for enhanced storm tracking.
May 3rd, 2025
🌩️ SPC Outlooks Page
Created Storm Prediction Center outlooks page with severe weather risk assessments and interactive maps.
May 4th, 2025
📱 Mobile Version
Launched mobile-optimized version of the Milton Weather Dashboard for enhanced mobile user experience.
May 4th, 2025
📊 Enhanced Forecast Features
Added dropdown functionality to forecast page and implemented seasonal countdown feature.
May 4th, 2025
✈️ METAR Data Page
Added METAR weather data page providing aviation weather information and detailed atmospheric conditions.
May 7th, 2025
🚨 Alerts Page
Launched comprehensive weather alerts page with real-time severe weather notifications and warnings.
May 7th, 2025
🏪 Station Comparison Redesign
Redesigned the compare weather stations modal with improved interface and enhanced data comparison features.
May 11th, 2025
🌊 Rip Current Risk Card
Added rip current risk assessment card with coastal safety information from National Weather Service.
May 20th, 2025
🌀 NHC Outlooks Page
Created National Hurricane Center outlooks page with tropical cyclone tracking and hurricane forecasts.
May 20th, 2025
🗺️ Weather Alert Maps
Added weather alert maps card with rotating severe weather, heat, and wind alert visualizations.
May 20th, 2025
⏰ Hourly Forecast Page
Launched detailed hourly forecast page providing hour-by-hour weather predictions and conditions.
May 20th, 2025
👥 Dedicated Users Page
Created dedicated users page to recognize and showcase the community supporting the weather dashboard.
May 26th, 2025
📈 Graphs Fully Functional
Completed implementation of interactive weather graphs for temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, and other metrics.
June 6th, 2025
🌧️ Rainfall Data Card
Added comprehensive rainfall data card with hourly, daily, monthly, and yearly precipitation totals.
June 6th, 2025
🔗 External Links
Added curated external links section providing access to additional weather resources and official meteorological services.
June 8th, 2025
🔄 NHC Outlooks Update
Updated National Hurricane Center outlooks page with improved functionality and enhanced tropical weather tracking.
June 28th, 2025
🔧 Site Maintenance
