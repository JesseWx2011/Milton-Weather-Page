May 3rd, 2025 Severe Weather Event

Return to Weather Event Log
Weather Event: Convective Severe Weather
Date: May 3, 2025
Severity: High
Location: Milton, FL

The evening of May 3rd, 2025, I'm watching a line of thunderstorms approaching from the west, and from my point of view that day, these storms looked quite alarming, and they were about to slam into where my weather station was. Lightning was a threat as well, as I am watching the storms in a nearby field, lightning struck a building right next to me.

That was my cue to run back to the station, quickly. Once again, lightning struck a building close to my position. I made it back, just minutes before it became extremely dangerous outside. The line of thunderstorms continued racing toward the station, as the winds accelerated quickly.

In a matter of minutes, the weather dramatically changed, the winds accelerated quickly, going from a shower to a downpour. It lasted for roughly 20 minutes, before eventually, the line of thunderstorms passed through.

Afterward, I got to see a cool-looking double-rainbow, and probably one of the best photos I have ever captured in my entire photography career, since I began in Mid-2022. What a day that was.
