April 26th 2025 Camp Okaloosa Floods

Weather Event: Flash Flooding
Date: April 26, 2025
Severity: Medium
Rainfall: Around 2 inches

On April 26th, 2025, in Northern Okaloosa county, a train of thunderstorms would train over Camp Okaloosa, where many, myself included, would be staying It was hot. It was muggy. It. Was. Exhausting. The high that day was forecasted to be in the lower 90s (Lower 30s in °C)

Shade wasn't providing much heat relief, only to cool us down by a degree.. barely. But we were very unaware of the events that were to occur that very day.

Around 4:30 PM, we began to hear the sound of thunder. I noticed the sky growing overcast. Very fast. But we figured it might be a small thundershower. But the atmosphere was very unstable that day. Just 30 minutes earlier, showers were just forming. Lightning became very frequent and would often strike less than a mile away.

9 Miles Away, to our south, a landspout had formed near Baker, but it would last a short 2 minutes before lifting. Back at the campsite, the rain got so heavy that we were forced into our tents. Less than a minute later, our tents were flodding. We decided to quickly pack our stuff and leave. All the trails were swamped in floodwater, making it difficult to walk through. We crossed a bridge and the creek was flodding bad. Very bad.

The same storm that produced the Camp Okaloosa flooding.

We made it back to the pavilion, and we learned that we were not alone. Many were in absolute shock after the event. The nearby field was flooded. Tents were swamped in floodwater. It was pure chaos. We left the campsite that day unharmed. While it could have been a wilder tale, it was the craziest camp of my life.
