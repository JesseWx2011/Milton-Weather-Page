June 19th, 2025 Gulf Shores, AL Waterspout

Return to Weather Event Log
Weather Event: Waterspout
Date: June 19, 2025
Severity: Moderate
Location: Gulf Shores, AL

The morning of June 19th, 2025, as I am headed out from where I was in Gulf Shores, I immediately spot a rainbow off to the west. As I head eastward, someone in the vehicle I was in spotted a funnel cloud, this turned out to be a waterspout, and I managed to take a couple photos of it just before it was dissipating.

Videos from earlier that day show the waterspout dissipating into a funnel just before it had reached the shoreline, I wasn't able to post it immediately, but after I got back, I made a post about it. Another video captured by someone else in the car also exists.
